# Lightweight Design Discovery

Use this only when design uncertainty can materially change the result. A local, well-defined change may proceed directly after inspecting the relevant code.

For an uncertain feature, capture: intended user outcome, smallest MVP, excluded scope, and one or two choices that need a decision. Present a short recommended approach and get confirmation only for material product or architecture choices.

Then implement the MVP and stop at the human acceptance gate. Do not require a formal specification, commit, multi-stage approval, or exhaustive plan before a normal first delivery.
